Welcome to Amistad!

You just spun home.

We are so excited to welcome you home. Our goal is to help make your transition into the Amistad FAMILY as smooth, comfortable, and memorable as possible.

Use this as your starting point, but know that you are never on this journey alone. The Welcome Committee is here to answer questions, help you find your way, and connect you with the people and resources you need.

We encourage you to jump in, engage, and build relationships. There truly is someone for everyone in Amistad. Here, you will find your people, your place, and your sense of belonging.
