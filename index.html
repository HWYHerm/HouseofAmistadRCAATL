Our Call & Response

Call: A · M · I   —   Response: S · T · A · D

It's the sound of every Amistad gathering — one voice calls out "A-M-I" and the whole House answers back "S-T-A-D." Ask any Amistadian and they'll teach you the hand signal that goes with it: a heart formed with crossed arms, echoing the crossed keys on our crest.

Our Name, Our Story

Friendship, forged in Spanish

Amistad is Spanish for friendship, and it's the name our House carries with pride. Amistad traces its heritage to Mexico — a house built from kind, intelligent, and fiercely competitive spirits. It took eight years to win a first House Championship, but that wait never dulled the fight in an Amistadian's heart.

"Once the peacock finds its feathers, it discovers who it really is."

Every student is challenged to find their own feathers — to grow past self-doubt and step fully into their greatness. Students who master the gifts, talents, and virtues of an Amistadian can earn the House's highest honor: knighthood — becoming protectors of the realm and guardians of its most valuable treasure, friendship itself.
